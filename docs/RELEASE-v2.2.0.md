# MS Astra 2.2.0

**Multi‑start exploration is now watched like a synthesis.** The background
worker streams every stage of every starting design — refinement frames,
needle scans with candidates, insertions, growth steps, cleanup, grid
enrichment and the final needle check — and the dashboard animates them: the
spectrum of the start being explored is drawn as a dashed blue line, its
coating structure and needle derivative on their charts, with the stage in
the status bar ("Start 7/10 «Ta₂O₅ 2 QWOT» · Cycle 3 · Thickness growth").
Finished starts stay on the spectrum as dashed grey lines, the leader as a
dashed green line; hovering a row of the starts list highlights it and shows
its structure. When the exploration ends, the winner is replayed with the
solid synthesis curve on top of the ghosts.

**Presets and Target moved into the Reflectance spectrum card.** The card has
three tabs: *Chart*, *Presets* (preset chips plus a summary of the current
task) and *Target* (spectral range, number of points, transition zone and
the target bands table). The sidebar keeps materials, starting design,
limits and advanced options and now fits without scrolling on a laptop.

**Elapsed‑time counter** in the Synthesis card: runs from Start through
exploration and replay, pauses with Pause, stops at completion.
